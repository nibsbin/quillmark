Scope: `crates/core/src/document/tests/` — `ambiguous_strings_tests.rs` (171 LOC, 6 tests), `assemble_tests.rs` (2169, 91), `card_fence_tests.rs` (314, 23), `edit_tests.rs` (1622, 81), `emit_idempotence_tests.rs` (96, 1), `emit_stability_tests.rs` (121, 1), `emit_tests.rs` (298, 13), `ext_tests.rs` (334, 13), `fence_conformance_tests.rs` (242, 2), `lossiness_tests.rs` (822, 30), `number_edge_tests.rs` (119, 3), `seed_tests.rs` (450, 17), `mod.rs` (12). Total ~6770 LOC, 281 tests. Cross-checked against `crates/core/tests/spec_conformance_probe.rs` (256, 19), `crates/core/tests/markdown_field_test.rs` (61, 2), `crates/fuzz/src/{parse_fuzz,emit_roundtrip_fuzz,coerce_fuzz,convert_fuzz}.rs`, and `crates/content/tests/properties.rs`.

Read first: `prose/references/markdown-spec.md` (§3, §8, §9/§9.1 are the contractual invariants — each deserves exactly one strong test, not zero, not six), `prose/canon/DOCUMENT_STORAGE.md` (Card-id/Anchor-id/Island-id identity sections state precise per-boundary policies: parse *repairs*, mutators and storage *reject* — these are three different contracts over one invariant, not three copies of the same check).

## Findings

### F1: The "N composable cards parse with correct kind/payload/order" shape is tested ~9 times in `assemble_tests.rs`
- **Category**: test-consolidation
- **Location**: `assemble_tests.rs:325` `test_multiple_card_blocks`, `:366` `test_mixed_global_and_cards`, `:574` `test_adjacent_blocks_different_kinds`, `:613` `test_order_preservation`, `:651-781` `test_product_catalog_integration` (131 lines), `:811` `test_quill_with_card_blocks`, `:1629` `test_card_consecutive_blocks`, `:1911` `test_spec_example`; also `card_fence_tests.rs:37` `card_fence_multiple_cards`
- **Evidence**: every one of these builds 2+ composable `~~~card-yaml` blocks and asserts the same predicate shape — `doc.cards().len() == N`, `cards()[i].kind() == Some(...)`, `cards()[i].payload().get(k).unwrap().as_str() == Some(...)`. E.g. `test_multiple_card_blocks` (325-363) and `test_card_consecutive_blocks` (1629-1648) differ only in field names (`name`/`tags` vs `id`); `test_adjacent_blocks_different_kinds` (574-610) and `test_mixed_global_and_cards` (366-399) differ only in whether the root carries extra payload fields. `test_product_catalog_integration` (651-781, the largest single test in the file) is a strict superset in shape of `test_multiple_card_blocks` + `test_adjacent_blocks_different_kinds` combined — it asserts nothing about interleaved kinds/global-payload/int-vs-float coercion that those two don't already cover individually, at ~4x the setup cost. `test_spec_example` (1911-1976) adds only "a `***` thematic break in the root body has no content representation" to the same base shape (already covered for `---` by `test_triple_dash_between_paragraphs_is_delegated`, F3).
- **Recommendation**: keep one table-driven test parameterized over `(markdown, expected_cards: &[(kind, field, value)])` covering: 2-cards-same-kind, 2-cards-different-kind, global-payload-plus-cards, and order-preservation-across-3. Delete `test_product_catalog_integration` and `test_spec_example` outright (no assertion in either isn't already covered by a smaller sibling or by F3's thematic-break tests). Keep `test_extended_metadata_demo_file` (1216, fixture-driven, distinct value: regression-checks a real checked-in Quill's demo file) separate.
- **Est. LOC removable**: ~280
- **Confidence**: high
- **Coverage risk**: none — the underlying "multiple composable cards split and parse independently" invariant has no single owner today (it's diffused across 9 tests); a single table test owns it more legibly than any one of the 9 does now.
