# 0.125 → 0.126 — a quill's font collections load, `status_report` stores enum ids, and script links, runaway YAML expansion and bodies nested past 12 levels are refused

The first break nothing reports: **a `.ttc` or `.otc` under `assets/fonts/` or
inside a vendored package loads every face it holds, where the backend skipped
it.** A plate naming a collection's family renders in it, where it rendered in
a fallback face and warned `unknown font family`. A quill whose only fonts are
collections renders in them, where it rendered in the embedded Roboto, so a
plate there naming `Roboto` warns `unknown font family`.

The second break: **the `status_report` fixture quill's `state` stores
`on_track`, `at_risk` and `blocked`, where it stored `on track`, `at risk` and
`blocked`.** The quill keeps `version: 0.1.0`. A document holding `state: at
risk` fails `validation::enum_violation` and does not render. Rewrite `on track`
to `on_track` and `at risk` to `at_risk`; the banner prints the same text.

A link whose target's scheme is `javascript:`, `vbscript:`, `data:` or `file:`
renders as its text alone under Typst, where it rendered a live link in PDF and
SVG. A card-yaml block whose expanded aliases and tags pass twice the block's
bytes plus 4 KiB of scalars, or the block's bytes plus 4 KiB of nodes, fails its
YAML parse, where it parsed.

**A body nests at most 12 levels deep, where it nested 100.** The list items,
quotes and elements open at one point of a body, and the marks open there,
count toward the bound (markdown spec §8). A body past it fails
`parse::body_import` at the body's `path`, `main.body` or
`cards.<kind>[i].body`, where it imported and failed to render once Typst's
show-rule depth ran out, by 16 quotes or 32 lists. Flatten the deepest levels.
`MAX_NESTING_DEPTH` is `12`, and `ParseError::BodyImport` holds
`{ path, message }`.

Under WASM, **a render that traps rejects as `runtime::backend_trapped`**, the
`WebAssembly.RuntimeError` on its `cause`, where it rejected as an uncoded
`attempted to take ownership of Rust value while it was borrowed`. A host
matching that message routes on the code, and discards the realm.
