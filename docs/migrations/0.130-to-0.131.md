# 0.130 → 0.131 — a top-level variant enum is a panel

**A card-level field outside every group, declared after a top-level `enum`
with `variants:`, warns `quill::field_after_object`.** An editor frames a
card-level variant enum as its own panel, as it frames a top-level `object`,
and draws every loose field above the panels. Declare the loose fields ahead
of it. The enum keeps its card-level key, so documents and plates read it
where they did.

| Break | Surface | Action |
|---|---|---|
| A loose field after a top-level variant enum warns `quill::field_after_object` | `Quill::warnings`, `quillmark validate` | Declare it ahead of the enum |
