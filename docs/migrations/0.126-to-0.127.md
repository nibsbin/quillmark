# 0.126 → 0.127 — an acroform field box that shows nothing fails to load

**An acroform quill whose `form.yaml` gives a field a `rect` with no width or
height, or one wholly off its page, fails to load as
`acroform::invalid_field_rect`, where it loaded and rendered with that field's
value nowhere to be seen.** The error names the field and its rect. A box that
only crosses its page's edge still loads. Give the box a positive `w` and `h`
on its page, or drop the field from `form.yaml`.

| Break | Surface | Action |
|---|---|---|
| A `form.yaml` field box with no area, or wholly off its page, fails to load as `acroform::invalid_field_rect` | acroform quill load | Fix the field's `rect`, or drop the field |
